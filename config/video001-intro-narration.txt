THE KUZ NETWORK is an open technology lab built around one idea: build local, keep control, own the stack.

Mini-Kuzai is one of its core projects: a language model developed from scratch, step by step, with its own data, training process, evaluations, and documented development flow.

This channel follows that journey.

Each video will present a verified milestone in a simple one-minute format, showing what was built, what was tested, and what comes next.

No marketing claims. No hidden cloud dependency. Just a transparent local AI project, developed openly and documented as it evolves.

Welcome to Mini-Kuzai. LLM from scratch.
